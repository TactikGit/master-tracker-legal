Master Tracker – Privacy Policy

Tactik Recycling Inc. · Last updated: September 25, 2026

Master Tracker is an internal tool of Tactik Recycling Inc. ("Tactik", "we"). It is used only by Tactik's authorized managers to review the company's own business activity. It is not offered to other companies or to the public.

What data the tool accesses

  • QuickBooks Online (Tactik's own company file): read-only access to accounting records such as invoices, bills, payments and their creation/modification dates and users.
  • Other Tactik systems: Tactik's own Zoho Creator records, Hubstaff time-tracking data and Microsoft 365 email counts (dates and times only, never email content).

How the data is used

The data is used only to show Tactik managers a summary of daily work activity. The tool does not modify QuickBooks data, does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Where the data is stored

The tool runs on a Tactik computer in Canada. Access credentials are stored locally on that computer and are not shared. Data read from QuickBooks is kept only temporarily in memory to display reports.

Who can see it

Only Tactik users with an account on the tool, and only the sections their role allows.

Employees

Tactik employees are informed of the work-activity information collected about them, in accordance with Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25). Employees may ask to see the information about them.

Disconnecting

Tactik can disconnect the tool from QuickBooks at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps). Access then stops immediately.
